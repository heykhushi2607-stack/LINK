The Key

Hey, I am the key you are yearning for. People find me scary and weird, but am I, though? If you look carefully, then you might stumble upon the only way to find out, which will have to be taken through those five transformations, in order. But if you are scared, let me tell you a tale from its past. It was waiting, waiting to be found, when someone changed it. Changed it to see a glance of me crystal clear. Changed it so I would feel familiar. Then, they placed me at the right place. Even then, they doubted themselves, because sometimes you need a reflection of your own to help. Right there, they found an echo of my sight. Only when focused do they see the real me, waiting to reveal? Reveal me only after you make us all of the same kind.
